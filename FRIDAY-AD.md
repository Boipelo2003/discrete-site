# One-day advert: "Websites from R350" (Friday 27 November 2026)

A pop-up advert for Boipelo Mothibi's web design offer. It shows when people open the shop, **only on Friday 27 November 2026 (South African time)**, and switches itself off at midnight.

- It shows once per visit (after the visitor closes it, it stays away until their next visit) and never on the cart, checkout, order or tracking pages.
- It is labelled "ADVERT" so customers know it is not a DISCRETE product.
- Buttons: WhatsApp chat with 069 320 5184 (with a ready message) and a Call button.

Change the words, price, number or dates in `lib/ad.js`. Nothing else needs touching.

To remove it later: delete the `<script src="js/ad.js" defer></script>` line from the pages, or just leave it (it does nothing outside the day).
